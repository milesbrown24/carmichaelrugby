The softer film-style pictures, phone screenshot and near-duplicate sequences were not added to the site. The uploaded hawkslogonew-1.svg is the original club logo used in the header and footer; it matches the SVG already obtained from the existing website.

Each selected photo has 480 px, 800 px and larger WebP variants with a JPEG fallback. Files are resized without upscaling and re-encoded for delivery. Metadata is removed, original color is preserved, and all original supplied files remain untouched. CSS controls the geometric crops; no players or photographic content were generated or altered.

The sharing image uses the supplied trophy team portrait. No event dates, tournament titles or results have been inferred from the photos.
